> C. Classifications
> 
> Taxonomy can make your head spin. It does mine whenever my
eyes light on an index of the [Universal Decimal Classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_Decimal_Classification)
(UDC). By what succession of miracles has agreement been
reached, practically throughout the world, that 668.184.2.099 shall
denote the finishing of toilet soap, and 629.1.018-465 horns on
refuse vehicles; whereas 621.3.027.23, 621.436:382, 616.24-002.5-
084, 796.54, and 913.15 denote respectively: tensions not exceeding
50 volts, the export trade in Diesel motors, the prophylaxy of
tuberculosis, camping, and the ancient geography of China and
Japan!

-- Georges Perec, [Think/Classify](https://info-design-lab.github.io/ID413-DataViz/files/Think%20Classify_Perec.pdf)
